# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Spice contributors and maintainers must make participation safe, constructive,
and professional. Expected behavior includes respectful technical disagreement,
clear evidence, patience with different experience levels, credit for others'
work, and prompt correction of mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing private
information, or using project access to retaliate against a contributor.

## Scope

This standard applies in Spice repositories, issue trackers, reviews,
discussions, community spaces, and public interactions where someone is acting
as a project representative.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the lead maintainer through GitHub. Do not
open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Maintainers will
review reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and take action
proportionate to the behavior and risk.

Possible actions include a private correction, warning, temporary participation
restriction, removal of repository or organization access, or a permanent ban.
Immediate restrictions may be used when needed to protect participants or the
project. A person subject to enforcement may request one written reconsideration
by a maintainer who was not the subject of the report when such a maintainer is
available.

Good-faith criticism of code, design, governance, or maintainer decisions is not
misconduct. Enforcement must not be used to suppress technical disagreement.
